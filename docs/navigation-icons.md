## Final prompt: Woodstock writing

Create a transparent PNG illustration asset for a minimalist personal blog navigation icon. Subject: Woodstock, the small yellow bird from Peanuts, sitting in profile and thoughtfully writing with a pencil in an open cream-paper notebook. Make the writing action obvious even when displayed at only 52x52 pixels. Compact square composition, full bird and notebook visible, centered with about 8% transparent margin. Style must match these existing Peanuts icons: warm off-white fills, golden yellow Woodstock, slightly irregular bold black hand-inked outlines, very subtle paper-like color texture, restrained flat comic coloring, friendly quiet expression. Tiny muted red notebook cover accent. No background, no ground rectangle, no lettering, no border, no drop shadow, no 3D, no glossy effects. All blank area outside character and notebook must have true transparent alpha. Asset type: illustration-story navigation icon. Save image locally for integrating into the website.

## Final prompt: Peanuts lineup

Use case: illustration-story. Create one horizontal transparent PNG navigation illustration for a personal blog brand. Peanuts comic friends standing together in a single friendly horizontal lineup: Lucy in a muted blue dress, Charlie Brown in yellow zigzag shirt, Snoopy white with black ears, small yellow Woodstock, and Linus in a muted red shirt holding his blue blanket. Five distinct recognizable characters, full bodies visible, lined up along one common baseline, quiet charming relaxed poses. Designed to replace a wordmark on hover at approximately 140 pixels wide by 50 pixels high: simple readable silhouettes and bold slightly wobbly hand-inked black outlines. Wide compact silhouette approximately 2.8:1, fill the canvas with only about 5 percent transparent safety margin; avoid large empty padding above and below. Match warm hand-drawn Peanuts comic icon set: cream/off-white, soft golden yellow, muted brick red, dusty blue, warm skin tones, very subtle paper texture in flat color fills. No text, no wordmark, no panel frame, no background scene, no ground shape, no cast shadow, no white sticker border, no shiny 3D shading. True transparent alpha outside characters and blanket. Preserve full heads, feet and hands. Do not generate a website mockup, just the wide standalone character lineup asset.
