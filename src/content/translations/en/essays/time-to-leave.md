Three years ago, the sky was bright.  
I pushed open an old wooden door. Voices everywhere.  
“So this is high school!” I thought.  
…  
Three years later, the sky was dark.  
I closed a new iron gate. Not a soul in sight.  
“So this was high school,” I thought.
