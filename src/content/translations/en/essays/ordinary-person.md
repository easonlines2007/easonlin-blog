Who does not want to succeed by the world’s standards?

The linear algebra test results came out today. Although it seems to have been a perfect case of choosing well mattering more than working hard (ha), I found an unexpected spring in my step on the way home.

For the past two weeks, it has been eight-to-five days without a break. I have also been seriously questioning whether I can manage studying in an unfamiliar language. An utterly unremarkable test result, but at least it steadied my nerves a little.

And then, rather ridiculously, I caught myself reflecting—or daydreaming? If, suppose, what if, one day I really did achieve that so-called worldly success, just how insufferably pleased with myself would I become?

…

It has poured in Barcelona all week. Today, at last, the sun came out.
